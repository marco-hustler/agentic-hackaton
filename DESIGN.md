## Brand & Style

**App name: Dividealo.** "Tactical Diorama" is the name of the design system; the product the user sees is always called **Dividealo**.

This design system translates the miniature delight, structured order, and tactical charm of simulation games (The Sims, SimCity) into an agentic asset-splitting and price-comparison interface. The aesthetic bridges tactile physical modeling with hyper-modern utility: modular floating cards, crisp structural grids, diorama-like inventory frames, and frosted observation viewports.

The UI evokes calm mastery during emotionally charged life transitions (settling shared property, parting ways, liquidating household items). It treats inventory resolution not as bureaucratic paperwork, but as an interactive miniature world where objects can be evaluated, distributed, or liquidated with absolute clarity and procedural calm.

Key visual attributes:
- **Tactile Toybox Precision:** Deep petrol structural elements ground warm terracotta, sage, and sand-toned modular items.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts treat screens like architectural blueprints or isometric stages, where cards and status badges float above subtle isometric ground planes.
- **Playful Rationalism:** Micro-interactions mimic snapping miniature pieces onto a grid, delivering instant satisfaction when decisions or valuations update.

## Colors

The palette balances tactical simulation authority with warm domestic grounding:

- **Primary (`#1E293B`):** Deep Petrol Slate. Used for primary typography, structural anchor bars, tactical toolbars, and decisive button states.
- **Secondary (`#E05A47`):** Vivid Terracotta Coral. Serves as the energetic action driver, valuation flag, and Idealo MCP agent trigger.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`):** Resilient Sage / Emerald. Reflects resolved states, fair splits, balanced household items, and peaceful allocations.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F6F0`):** Architectural Cream. Provides a soft, warm paper/ground-tile base, avoiding the cold clinical look of pure white.

### Extended Semantic Roles
- **Ground Tile / Canvas Dark:** `#0F172A` (Midnight Slate) used for tactical diorama preview layers and header docks.
- **Warm Clay Surface:** `#EAE0D5` used for secondary card backing, table headers, and structural divides.
- **MCP Agent Sky:** `#0284C7` (Sky Slate Blue) reserved strictly for live agentic processes, API price-polling indicators, and automated Idealo scraping badges.
- **Caution / Draft:** `#D97706` (Sim Amber) for pending reviews and contested items.

## Typography

The type system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, marrying geometric clarity with warm, humanist apertures. The typeface evokes the approachable crispness of a high-end simulation HUD.

- **Scale & Hierarchy:** Numerics and monetary valuations lean into bold and extra-bold weights (`700`, `800`) to evoke real-time game counters. Headlines use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic condensed toy packaging or architectural legends.
- **Labels & Micro-Metrics:** All status indicators, MCP telemetry tags, and item category badges are set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight uppercase letter-spacing to ensure legibility when overlaid on frosted glass surfaces.
- **Form Factors:** Mobile sizes step down sharply beyond `headline-md` to avoid awkward word wraps in compact inventory columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout treats mobile portrait view as a tactile inventory inspector:

- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column fluid system on mobile screens, scaling to an 8-column layout on tablets and a centered 12-column stage (max width 1120px) on desktop viewports.
- **Safe Viewport & Stage:** The upper canvas hosts the dynamic room / inventory visualization stage, while the lower two-thirds house scrollable, interlocking decision cards.
- **Modular Rhythm:** Layout gaps follow strict multiples of 4px. Component internal padding uses `space-md` (`0.75rem`) for compact telemetry and `space-xl` (`1.5rem`) for hero decision cards.
- **Reflow Principles:** On mobile, split-balance meters remain sticky to the top or bottom edge as a tactile floating bar; secondary metadata drops below the item card rather than sitting in adjacent table cells.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical miniature staging: crisp, frosted white cards layered over tactile earthy surfaces, paired with directional ambient drop shadows.

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** Warm cream `#F8F6F0` textured with micro-grid points or subtle isometric tiles. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Diorama Stage Plates):** Warm clay `#EAE0D5` or midnight slate `#0F172A` inset surfaces. Styled with an inner hairline border (`1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) and a sharp, tactile 1px baseline shadow (`0 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Cards):** Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` or semi-opaque frosted panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Elevated via a dual shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 2px 4px 0 rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`, framed by a delicate border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on light, `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)` on dark).
- **Level 3 (Tactile Control Pills & Modals):** High-interaction buttons and floating split docks receive a punchy directional drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(224, 90, 71, 0.24)` for terracotta actions, or `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)` for slate anchors.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 Roundedness** to mirror milled game pieces, smooth wooden tokens, and rounded plastic diorama accessories:

- **Base Cards & Modules:** `rounded` (`0.5rem`) for compact metadata tiles and sub-elements; `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) for primary inventory cards, balance summary containers, and bottom sheets.
- **Pills & Status Micro-Units:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) for status tags, Idealo MCP lookup chips, agent status pings, and balance indicator tokens.
- **Tactile Notches:** Interactive toggle buttons and slider tracks feature deep rounded insets to reinforce the feel of physical mechanical switches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tactical Action (Terracotta):** Solid `#E05A47` with white text. High tactile presence: 1px border (`#C94A38`), subtle top inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`), and a firm physical press animation (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary Neutral (Petrol Slate):** Background `#1E293B`, crisp white text, pairing high contrast with rounded pill or `rounded-md` silhouette.
- **Agent Action (Idealo MCP):** Translucent sky blue base (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.1)`) with `#0284C7` text, 1px border (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.3)`), and a pulsing dot indicator to denote live web-agent valuation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Compact height (24px to 28px), `label-sm` weight, uppercase tracking.
  - *Fair / Settled:* Soft sage background (`#D1FAE5`) with deep emerald text (`#065F46`).
  - *Contested / Draft:* Sand-amber tint (`#FEF3C7`) with warm amber text (`#92400E`).
  - *MCP Live Valued:* Frosted ice blue (`rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.9)`) with `#0369A1` text and an integrated miniature spark icon.

### Cards & Diorama Modules
- **Inventory Item Card:** Crisp white surface with frosted overlay, featuring a left-anchored square thumbnail mimicking an isometric 3D asset box. Right-hand section displays title, Idealo fair-market valuation, and a segmented dual-avatar pill for item allocation (Party A / Party B / Liquidate).
- **Split Balance Header Card:** Fixed or sticky floating widget showcasing a dynamic horizontal balance bar (terracotta on the left, sage on the right, balanced needle in the center).

### Inputs & Controls
- **Numeric Valuation Field:** Monospaced-style numeric display inside an architectural pill. Clear active focus outline in `#E05A47` (`0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 90, 71, 0.2)`).
- **Segmented Allocation Sliders:** Recessed clay track (`#EAE0D5`) housing an extruded white pill slider thumb that snaps to allocation positions with immediate tactile feedback.

### Lists
- Dividers between inventory rows use subtle 1px dashed or dotted rules (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`), reminiscent of architectural floor-plan grid lines.
